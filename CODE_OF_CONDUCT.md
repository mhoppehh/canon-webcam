# Code of conduct

## 1. Our standard

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. Read it at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short:

- Be patient and polite. Assume that the other person wants the software to work.
- Discuss the code, not the person.
- Accept that a report about a camera, a computer or a driver can be correct even
  when it contradicts your own test.
- Do not publish another person's private data. This includes log files with a
  serial number or a password.

## 2. Scope

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and
any other space of this project.

## 3. Report a problem

Send a private message to the maintainer of the repository. Give the place, the
date and what happened. The maintainer keeps the report private.

The maintainer can remove a comment, close a conversation or block a person who
does not follow this standard.
